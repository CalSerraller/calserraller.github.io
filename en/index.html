Cal Serraller is a rural holiday home in Miravet, in the Ribera d'Ebre area of Catalonia, for up to six guests. It is a family-owned village house and we take care of every detail so your stay feels as comfortable as home.

The ground floor is a large open-plan room for get-togethers, games or simply relaxing. The kitchen and dining room share one open, spacious and fully equipped space for unhurried meals. The entrance has a private patio.

The house has three floors: the open-plan room on the ground floor, the open living and dining room with the kitchen on the first floor, and the three bedrooms and a full bathroom on the second.

It's about a 5-minute walk from the village centre and the shops (butcher, supermarket and bakery). From the house you can explore the village, the castle and the Ebro river, and discover the landscapes and activities of the Terres de l'Ebre.

Frequently asked questions

Where is Cal Serraller?

On Raval dels Canterers, in the village of Miravet (43747), in the Ribera d'Ebre area of the Terres de l'Ebre, Tarragona, Catalonia.

Is it a rural holiday home?

It is a whole village house for up to 6 guests, registered as a holiday rental (HUTTE-077394). If you are looking for a rural holiday home in Miravet for your family or friends, you get the whole house to yourselves: three floors, a patio with a barbecue and the village centre a 5-minute walk away.

How many people does it sleep?

Up to 6 people, in 3 bedrooms: 1 double bed and 4 single beds.

What is the minimum stay?

2 nights. Stays of 4 nights or more get 10% off when you book direct.

Where can I park?

On the street, for free, on Raval dels Canterers, right outside the house or a few metres away.

What are the check-in and check-out times?

Check-in is from 4 pm to 9 pm and check-out is by 12 noon. Please let us know your arrival time in advance.

Is it suitable for children?

Yes. There are board games, books, scooters and bikes, and a high chair and cot on request.

Can I bring my dog?

Yes, pets are welcome.

How far is it from the village centre?

About a 5-minute walk from the centre and the shops: butcher, supermarket and bakery.

Can I work remotely from the house?

Yes. There's high-speed Wi-Fi throughout the house and a workspace.

Are bed linen and towels included?

Yes, cotton bed linen and towels for every guest.
